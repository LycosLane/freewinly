# FreeWinly Brand Guidelines

## Core Identity

FreeWinly uses the Fluke Fedora as its official mark. The symbol unites the White Fedora of the open source software suite (representing transparent, ethical, zero-bloat software) with a breaching whale tail fluke (a nod to "FreeWinly" and freedom from Windows bloatware).

## Logo Geometry

The logo is built from flat geometric vector shapes:
- **Angle**: A dynamic -6 degree upward tilt matching the suite family.
- **Crown**: Crisp white silhouette with a negative-space V-pinch at the top.
- **Whale Tail Fluke**: Nested inside the top V-pinch crease, replacing the standard diamond pinch with a symmetrical breaching whale tail fluke in Brand Cyan (`#00B4FF`).
- **Negative Space Gap**: A clean horizontal cut separating the crown from the ribbon, showing the `#101216` background directly.
- **Modular Ribbon**: Two color blocks separated by negative space:
  - Left segment (Windows Blue `#0078D4`): Symbolizes native Windows OS tooling and stability.
  - Right segment (Brand Cyan `#00B4FF`): Symbolizes clean caches, speed, and healthy storage.
- **Brim**: Bold, sweeping white curve running across the base of the mark.

| Brand Cyan | Clean Cyan | `#00B4FF` | `0, 180, 255` | Breaching whale fluke and secondary ribbon slice |

## Suite Family Alignment

The White Fedora mark serves as the universal anchor across the open source suite:
- **FreeDataDiet**: Cyan (`#00B4FF`) and Amber (`#FFB020`) ribbon with white diamond crown pinch.
- **FreeWinly**: Windows Blue (`#0078D4`) and Cyan (`#00B4FF`) ribbon with breaching whale tail fluke in the crown pinch.
- **Alltimer**: Deep Indigo (`#6366F1`) and Purple (`#A855F7`).

## Usage Rules
- Do not add 3D bevels, glossy reflections, or drop shadows to the hat or fluke vectors.
- Maintain the -6 degree slant. Do not rotate arbitrarily.
- Keep the negative-space V-pinch and band gap transparent to the `#101216` squircle background.
